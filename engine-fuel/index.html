This calculator uses 2026/27 UK income tax and National Insurance rates for England, Wales and Northern Ireland (Scotland’s income tax bands differ) and a simplified model of pension tax relief. It doesn’t account for pension unused annual allowance carryover, your personal tax code, student loan repayments or other deductions.

A ‘Not salary sacrifice’ pension contribution could be made via Relief at Source. This is simplified in the calculator. Under Relief at Source, only 20% relief is added automatically, with higher and additional rate taxpayers reclaiming the rest through Self Assessment rather than seeing it reflected in take home pay straight away, as they would under a Net Pay Arrangement. This calculator shows the full relief immediately.
